About HumAIn

AI explained like a human.

HumAIn is a faceless, English-language YouTube channel (@HumAInExplained, launched October 26, 2026) that explains AI in plain words, with real tests instead of hype.

This website is the home for everything that goes with the videos: the Agent Library, the Prompt Library, and the free course.

What we believe

  • Humans first. If an explanation needs a glossary, we rewrite the explanation.
  • Test it, then tell the truth. When an AI tool makes a mistake on camera, we show it. In our $40 dinner video, we added up the numbers ourselves.
  • Sources over vibes. We cite official pages for prices, plans and limits, and say when we checked them. We don't invent statistics.
  • Safety is part of the lesson. Privacy, scams and prompt injection are in the curriculum, not the footnotes.
  • Free means free. The libraries and the course cost nothing. The course asks for an email so we can build a list of people who want to hear from us, and you can leave at any time.

The “robot intern”

Our mascot is a friendly robot intern: fast, keen, and in need of supervision. It's how we think about AI agents: give them clear tasks, limited access, and always check their work.

Independent

HumAIn is independent. It's not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Zapier, Make, beehiiv or YouTube. We link to official sources so you can verify what we say.
