Privacy & email consent

Last updated: November 10, 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What this website stores

  • In your browser only: a “signed up” flag and your lesson progress (browser localStorage). They never leave your device. “Sign out of this device” on the course page clears them.
  • No cookies or analytics are set by this website at the time of writing. If that changes we'll update this page.
  • Hosting: the site is served from our own virtual server (OVH), which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security and troubleshooting.

When you sign up by email

  • For the general newsletter (when it opens), your email address is collected and stored by our email provider, beehiiv, under its own privacy policy. The Intermediate Course works differently; see the next section.
  • We use it to send you the emails you agreed to: free course updates, new videos, and new agents and prompts.
  • We don't sell your email address.
  • You can withdraw consent and unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to ian-remi.lafleur@neotechcloudsolutions.com.

The Intermediate Course (by email only)

The 11-lesson basic course on this site needs no sign-up. The free Intermediate Course is sent only by email, so it needs your address and your consent.

  • What we store: your email address, the date and wording of your consent, a shortened version of your IP address (last part removed) as proof of that consent, and the dates you confirmed or unsubscribed. We first send one confirmation email (double opt-in); nothing else is sent until you press the confirm button.
  • Personal lesson links and visit tracking. Every course email contains a link that is unique to you (for example humainexplained.com/c/<your-code>/lesson-1). The code is tied to your email address in our own database. When that link is opened, we record the lesson, the date and time, and a shortened version of your browser's identifier (user agent). We do not store your IP address with these visits, and we use no cookies or third-party analytics for this. We use it to understand whether people open the lessons and which ones are useful. It shows whether a given subscriber opened a given lesson; it does not follow you elsewhere on the web and does not record other pages you visit on this site. Some mail programs and security scanners open links automatically; we try to filter those out, but the numbers are approximate. Please don't share your personal link.
  • Email delivery: course emails are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which acts on our behalf and receives your email address and the message so it can deliver it. Your data may therefore be processed outside Quebec and Canada. We turn off Brevo's own open and click tracking for course emails, so your personal links are not rewritten by a third party.
  • How long we keep it: while you are subscribed and for up to 12 months after you unsubscribe (we then keep only your address and the unsubscribe date, so we don't email you again). Visit records are deleted together with your subscriber record.
  • Unsubscribe and deletion: every email has an unsubscribe link (one click, then a confirmation button). Unsubscribing stops the emails and closes your personal link. To have all your data deleted, including your visit history, write to ian-remi.lafleur@neotechcloudsolutions.com and we will erase it.
  • Who can see it: only the site owner, through a password-protected admin page on our own server, and the delivery service above. We don't sell your data.

We only email you if you actively tick the consent box (it's never pre-ticked) and sign up yourself. Every email identifies the sender and includes a working unsubscribe link. Third-party links

Videos and sources open on other websites (YouTube and official help pages) that have their own policies.

The course sign-up is an honour-system gate on a static website. It is built to grow our email list, not to lock content away. See the README for details.
