Privacy & email consent

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Who we are

HumAIn is run by NeoTech Cloud Solutions, 2572 boul. Daniel-Johnson, 2nd floor, office 237, Laval, QC H7T 2R3, Canada. For any privacy question or request, write to ian-remi.lafleur@neotechcloudsolutions.com. That address reaches the person in charge of protecting personal information at HumAIn.

What this website stores

  • In your browser only: your lesson progress for the basic course (browser localStorage). It never leaves your device, and you can clear it from the course page.
  • No cookies or analytics are set by this website at the time of writing. If that changes we'll update this page.
  • The basic course, the Agent Library and the Prompt Library need no sign-up.
  • Hosting: the site and its email-course database run on a virtual server rented from OVH, located in Beauharnois, Quebec, Canada. The server may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

When you sign up for our emails

Signing up is optional and needs your express consent: a box you tick yourself, never pre-ticked. We first send a confirmation email (double opt-in); nothing else is sent until you press the confirm button.

  • What we collect and store: your email address, the date, version and wording of the consent you gave, a shortened version of your IP address (last part removed) as proof of that consent, and the dates you confirmed or unsubscribed.
  • Why we use it (purposes):
    • to send you the free Intermediate Course: your personal course link, and reminders about every two days, only for lessons you haven’t opened yet, until you finish or unsubscribe;
    • to send occasional emails about new videos, agents and prompts;
    • to send occasional emails about HumAIn’s paid offers, such as self-paced labs and advanced courses, when they are available;
    • to understand whether lessons are opened and useful (see personal lesson links below);
    • to keep proof of your consent and to honour your unsubscribe.
  • We never sell your data and we don't use it for anything else.

Personal lesson links and visit tracking

Every course email contains a link that is unique to you (for example humainexplained.com/c/<your-code>/lesson-1). The code is tied to your email address in our own database. When that link is opened, we record the lesson, the date and time, and a shortened version of your browser's identifier (user agent). We do not store your IP address with these visits, and we use no cookies or third-party analytics on our site for this. (Brevo's separate, anonymous email open and click statistics are described under “Where your data is stored, who handles it” below.) It shows whether a given subscriber opened a given lesson; it does not follow you elsewhere on the web and does not record other pages you visit on this site. Some mail programs and security scanners open links automatically; we try to filter those out, but the numbers are approximate. Please don't share your personal link.

Where your data is stored, who handles it

  • Our server: subscriber records and visits are kept in a SQLite database on our OVH virtual server in Beauharnois, Quebec, Canada. A backup is made every day and the last 14 daily backups are kept on the same server; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Email delivery: course emails are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which acts on our behalf and receives your email address and the message so it can deliver it. Your data may therefore be processed outside Quebec and Canada. Brevo records email opens and link clicks in anonymous form, meaning they are not associated with individual contacts. Brevo does not let us switch this tracking off completely, so the links in our emails pass through a Brevo redirect before they reach humainexplained.com, and a small hidden image may be used to detect that an email was opened. This is separate from our own visit tracking described above, which is unchanged.
  • General newsletter: if and when a general newsletter opens, its sign-up may be handled by another provider (beehiiv), and this page will say so before it starts.
  • Who can see it: only the site owner, through a password-protected admin page, and the delivery service above.

How long we keep it

Your record and visit log are kept while you are subscribed and for up to 12 months after you unsubscribe. After unsubscribing we keep only your address and the unsubscribe date, so we don't email you again; then everything is deleted. Visit records are deleted with your subscriber record. Deleted data disappears from our backups within 14 days.

Your rights (Quebec Law 25 and other Canadian laws)

  • Access: ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correction: ask us to fix inaccurate information.
  • Deletion and withdrawing consent: unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email (one click, then a confirmation button), or write to ian-remi.lafleur@neotechcloudsolutions.com. On request we erase all your data, including your visit history.
  • How to ask: email the address above. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
