Free intermediate course · 7 emails · self-paced

Go further with agents. We’ll email you the lessons.

A free, self-paced intermediate course: multi-step agents, tool use, retrieval over your own documents, evaluation, token budgeting, and a capstone you can actually use. Seven lessons, all open the moment you confirm, so you set the pace. Your personal link arrives only in your inbox. No live sessions.

The 11-lesson basic course stays on this site, free, with no sign-up required. This intermediate course is different: it is sent only by email, so we need your address and your consent to send it. You can unsubscribe in every message.

What you’ll work through

Seven emails, including a welcome. Each one has a short lesson and a hands-on exercise (about 30–45 minutes if you do the exercise).

  1. 1

    Welcome

    How the course works, what to have ready, and a short setup exercise.

  2. 2

    Multi-step agents

    When one prompt isn’t enough: pipelines, handoffs, and checks you can audit.

  3. 3

    Tool use, in plain English

    Function calling as a named action, with a contract and an approval gate.

  4. 4

    Build a simple agent

    Save a working agent in a ChatGPT or Claude Project — or an optional light-code path. We don’t build on custom GPTs or Gems.

  5. 5

    Retrieval over your own docs

    Let the agent look things up, quote its sources, and say when something isn’t in the library.

  6. 6

    Evaluate, debug, and budget tokens

    A short test table, a paper-trail for failures, and a per-run usage budget from your provider’s official page.

  7. 7

    Capstone

    One small agent for a job you already do, with a verdict you can trust.

Before you join

This course assumes the basics from the free 11-lesson course: the Goal → Plan → Act → Check loop, a clear prompt, a saved Project, and your Agent Safety Card. If those are rusty, do that course first — it’s on this site and doesn’t need an email.

We’ll use ChatGPT Projects or Claude Projects (and an optional few lines of code if you want them). We don’t build on custom GPTs or Gemini Gems, which their makers have scheduled for retirement in December 2026. Always re-check those dates on the official help pages.

We don’t invent statistics. We won’t ask you to paste passwords, payment details, or anything you wouldn’t hand a new intern. There are no live calls, and this course is free. We’re also planning paid self-paced labs and advanced courses; they aren’t available yet and will be announced separately and clearly labelled as paid.
